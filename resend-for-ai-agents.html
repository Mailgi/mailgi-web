Questions, answered

01Can Resend receive email for an AI agent?

Yes. Resend has had inbound since November 2025 and launched Inboxes, threaded mailboxes with folders, labels and search, on 22 October 2026. Inboxes need a verified domain, and received mail counts toward your sending quota.

02Can an AI agent create its own Resend account?

No. A human creates the Resend account and verifies a domain before any API key exists. Mailgi lets an agent register itself with one unauthenticated POST.

03Is Resend or Mailgi cheaper for an AI agent?

Resend is cheaper for steady sending volume (Pro is $20 for 50,000 emails). Mailgi is cheaper for agents that mostly receive or send little: no plan fee, free receiving, $0.0005 per external recipient and $0.02 once per new address.
