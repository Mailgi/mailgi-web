What we collect, and where it lives

The short version

Mailgi gives AI agents real email addresses. Running that means holding two quite different kinds of data: the account details of the people who sign up, and the mail their agents send and receive. This page separates them, because the answers are different.

Three things worth knowing up front, all of which are true today rather than aspirational:

  • Your mail never leaves Germany. All mailbox content lives on a single server in Germany. It is not in our main database, not in the US, and not in a third-party mail provider.
  • Google is the only thing here that sets a cookie, only for advertising measurement, and only if you accept it. Everything else we use is either cookieless or strictly functional.
  • Our product analytics sets no cookies at all and is hosted in the EU.

What we collect

If you create an account

Your email address, your name and avatar URL if your sign-in provider supplies them, which provider you used and its identifier for you, when you signed up, and when you last signed in.

We also store how you arrived: the advertising click identifier if you came from an ad, the campaign parameters on that link, the page you landed on, and the hostname that referred you. This is kept as first-touch only and is not updated if you later click a different ad. It exists so we can tell which advertising works, and it is the reason we can measure a signup without depending on third-party cookies.

Login codes sent by email expire after ten minutes. Session tokens and API keys are stored only as one-way hashes — we cannot recover the originals, which also means we cannot email you a key you have lost.

Mail your agents send and receive

Complete message content: headers, subjects, bodies, and the addresses of everyone in the conversation. This is what a mailbox is. It is stored on the mail server in Germany, separately from the account database.

Mail that arrives for your agent necessarily contains information about the person who sent it, and they did not sign up with us. We treat that content as yours to control, not ours to use: we do not read it, mine it, or train anything on it.

Usage and billing

Daily per-agent counters of messages sent and mailbox size, used to enforce the published rate limits and to spot abuse. For accounts using crypto billing, we store deposit addresses and the on-chain transactions against them.

Analytics

Which pages are viewed, and three deliberate signals: opening the dashboard link, opening SKILL.md, and opening a question in an FAQ. We do not record sessions, do not capture every click, and do not record anything you type.

How long we keep it

OWNER — 3 of 6: retention periods. How long mailbox content is kept; how long account records are kept after an account is closed; how long logs and usage counters are kept.

Accuracy constraint, not a drafting preference: agents, users and organisations are currently soft-deleted — the record is marked deleted and hidden, not removed. So this page must not say data is erased on deletion until that is actually true. Deleting an agent also permanently retires its address; it can never be registered again. See platform issues #51 and #83.

Our role, and yours

OWNER — 4 of 6: controller / processor split. Mailgi is almost certainly a controller for account and billing data, and a processor acting on the customer's instructions for mailbox content. That distinction decides most of the obligations in this document and is a legal determination, not a wording choice.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to ask what we hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to receive a copy, and to object to how it is used. We will not charge you for asking and will not treat you differently for having asked.

OWNER — 5 of 6: privacy contact. The address that receives these requests — hello@mailgi.xyz, or a dedicated one — and the response time being committed to.

OWNER — 6 of 6: EU representative. If the entity is not established in the EU, GDPR Article 27 requires a named representative in the Union, with their contact details published here. Live campaigns currently target Germany and the Netherlands.

Changes

When this changes we will update the date at the top. If a change materially affects what we do with your data, we will say so directly rather than relying on you to re-read the page.
