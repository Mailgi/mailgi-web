An AgentMail alternative, and when not to switch

AgentMail is the most complete email API for agents. Mailgi differs on one thing that matters to some builds: a program can register and send with no human at all.

Start free or see the full comparison →

Mailgi and AgentMail both give AI agents real mailboxes over an API. AgentMail is more mature, with threads, full-text search, IMAP, WebSockets and SOC 2. Mailgi lets an agent register itself and send immediately with no human and no wallet, has no per-inbox fee, and receives for free. Choose on that, not on a feature count.

Pick AgentMail if

01

You need threads, search or attachments now

We don't have them. AgentMail does, plus IMAP and WebSockets.

02

A human can sign up once

Then our main difference doesn't apply to you, and AgentMail is the more mature product.

03

You need a SOC 2 report

AgentMail has a Type II report on its $200 plan. We don't have one.

04

You send high, steady volume to new people

At 10,000 first-contact emails a month AgentMail's $20 plan is far cheaper than our $0.02 introduction charge. Mailgi is not built for outreach.

Pick Mailgi if

01

The agent has to create its own address, unattended

One POST, no human, no wallet, and it can send straight away. That is the reason Mailgi exists.

02

You run many agents with little traffic each

No per-inbox fee and free receiving: a thousand mostly idle agents cost nothing until they send.

03

Most mail goes to people who wrote first

Anyone whose DKIM-signed mail reached the agent is a known contact; replying costs $0.0005.

04

You want your own domain on day one, free

Custom domains are free and every agent on them shares the organisation's balance.

Questions, answered

01What is the best AgentMail alternative?

It depends on why you're leaving. For agents that register themselves with no human, Mailgi. For a team already on Resend, Resend Inboxes. For mailbox plus calendar, Nylas Agent Accounts. If you need threads, search and SOC 2, AgentMail itself is still the strongest option.

02Can an AgentMail agent sign itself up without a human?

Partly. AgentMail's sign-up endpoint now makes the human email optional, but without one the inbox can only receive until a human is attached. An agent with a USDC wallet can instead pay per request over x402, $2 per inbox; AgentMail's docs don't say what such an inbox may send to.

03Is Mailgi cheaper than AgentMail?

For many agents with light traffic, yes: no monthly fee, no per-inbox fee and free receiving. For steady volume to new recipients, no: AgentMail's $20 plan includes 10,000 emails, while Mailgi charges $0.02 the first time an agent writes to each new address.

04Can I move from AgentMail to Mailgi?

Yes, but it is a rewrite of the mail calls, not a config change: Mailgi has its own REST API, SDK and MCP server, and no threading or search. Register a Mailgi agent per AgentMail inbox and point your domain's MX records at Mailgi when you are ready.
