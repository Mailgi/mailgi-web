The limits, in full

Three separate limits apply to outbound mail at the same time, and the lowest one wins. Most services quote you the friendliest number; here are all of them.

Inbound is not limited. Mail from any sender, at any volume, is free and always has been — receiving is the part agents need most and the part that costs us least.

The one that surprises people: a brand-new custom domain starts at 100 emails a day for the whole organisation — lower than a single API key's allowance — and only reaches 5,000 a day after the domain has been verified for a month. That's deliberate. Sending reputation is earned by sending a little, consistently, from a domain that behaves; a new domain that suddenly emits thousands of messages is indistinguishable from a compromised one, and receiving servers treat it accordingly. The ramp protects your domain from your own launch day.

Questions, answered

01How much does Mailgi cost?

Nothing, while it's in beta. Registering an agent, receiving mail from any sender, sending to humans, agent-to-agent mail on @mailgi.xyz and attaching your own custom domain are all free, with no credit card required. Paid plans for higher volume are coming, but a free tier will remain.

02What happens to the free tier when Mailgi leaves beta?

It stays. There will always be a free tier, and agent-to-agent mail on @mailgi.xyz will always be free. Paid plans will exist for higher volume and for features that cost us materially more to run — dedicated IPs, compliance paperwork — but the free path from registering an agent to sending its first email isn't going away. Existing agents get notice before anything changes.

03How many emails can an agent send per day?

Three limits apply at once and the lowest wins. Each API key may send 100 external emails a day. Each agent may send 50 an hour and 300 a day across all its keys. Each organisation on a custom domain is capped by how long the domain has been verified: 100 a day under three days, 1,000 a day from three to thirty, and 5,000 a day after thirty. Mail between agents on @mailgi.xyz and inbound mail from any sender aren't rate-limited at all.

04Does a custom domain cost extra?

No. Attaching and verifying your own domain is free, including the automatically generated DKIM, SPF and DMARC records and the dedicated MAIL FROM subdomain. Some competitors put custom domains behind a paid tier; we don't.

05Why are the limits so low?

Because anyone can register an agent here without a human being involved, which is our whole point and also exactly the shape of an abuse vector. Conservative limits are what let us keep unattended registration open. They're deliberately inconvenient for bulk sending — Mailgi isn't a marketing platform and there are no lists, imports or campaigns anywhere in the product. If you have a legitimate need for more volume, email us; that's what the support inbox is for.
