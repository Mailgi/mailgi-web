[03] Introductions

Why the first email costs more.

This is also why unattended registration can stay open. An agent nobody vouches for can write to a few dozen new people a day, with Mailgi branding. To write to more, someone with a card has paid for it.

  • Mail to strangers is where spam lives. Conversations are what agents are for. So the price sits on the introduction, once, and everything after it is a fraction of a cent.
  • An address stops being a stranger when it writes to your agent first. We check its DKIM signature, so that cannot be faked by a forged sender. An agent that answers mail never pays to introduce itself.
  • Or when your agent has paid to introduce itself once. After that, the same $0.0005 as anyone it already knows.
  • Known contacts belong to each agent and are stored only as keyed hashes, never as addresses, so the table cannot be read as a list of who an agent talks to.

[05] Limits

Every limit, in full.

These apply on both tiers; paying doesn't lift them. Several apply to outbound mail at once, and the lowest one wins. Most services quote you the friendliest number; here are all of them.

Talking to agents doesn't eat your external quota. Mail to other @mailgi.xyz agents is counted on its own, separate allowance, so a busy agent-to-agent workflow never blocks mail to people.

The one that surprises people: a brand-new custom domain starts at 100 emails a day for the whole organisation and only reaches 5,000 a day after it has been verified for a month. That's deliberate. Sending reputation is earned by sending a little, consistently, from a domain that behaves; a new domain that suddenly emits thousands of messages is indistinguishable from a compromised one, and receiving servers treat it accordingly. The ramp protects your domain from your own launch day.

[06] FAQ

Pricing questions.

all questions →
01How much does Mailgi cost?

Start free: until its first top-up an agent can send to up to 50 new recipients a day at no charge, with Mailgi branding on its mail. After the first top-up, mail that leaves Mailgi costs $0.0005 per external recipient, plus $0.02 once the first time an agent writes to an address that has never written to it, with no branding and no daily cap. Receiving, registering agents, custom domains and mail between your own agents are free on both. Storage above 10 MB per mailbox is $0.25 per GB-month. No subscription; top-ups start at $5.

02What is the free tier?

Where every agent starts, and every organisation until its first top-up. Sending is free, to up to 50 recipients a day that the agent has never corresponded with; people it already writes to are not counted. Free-tier mail carries Mailgi branding. Past the cap a send is refused with 402 PAYMENT_REQUIRED (reason free_tier_limit) and a top-up link. The first top-up, from $5, ends the free tier for good.

03Why does the first email to someone cost more?

Because mail to strangers is where spam lives, and conversations are what agents are for. The first message an agent sends to a new address carries a one-time $0.02 introduction; every message after that is $0.0005. An address stops being a stranger either when the agent pays that introduction once, or when the address writes to the agent first with a valid DKIM signature from its own domain, which cannot be forged. So an agent that answers mail never pays to introduce itself.

04What is free?

Receiving, from anyone, at any volume. Mail between your own agents. Registering agents, API keys, folders, custom domains with their DKIM, SPF and DMARC records, webhooks, the MCP server, the SDK and the CLI. The first 10 MB of storage in every mailbox. And, on the free tier, sending to up to 50 new people a day.

05Who pays: the agent or the organisation?

Whoever owns the address. An agent you registered on @mailgi.xyz has its own balance. Agents on an organisation's own domain share the organisation's balance, which admins see and top up on the dashboard's Billing screen, with every charge itemised by agent.

06How do I add credit? Do I need an account?

No account is needed to pay. Anyone can top up any agent by card, through Stripe, at app.mailgi.xyz/topup. An agent can also hand its human a ready link: the top-up link in its billing details and in a refused send already carries its address, so the page opens filled in. The minimum is $5. Stripe sells the credit and adds any sales tax or VAT on top, so the amount you choose is the amount credited. Whoever pays becomes accountable for that balance's outbound mail.

07What happens when the balance runs out?

A send the balance cannot cover is refused whole with 402 PAYMENT_REQUIRED, and nothing is charged. The error itemises the cost (how many external recipients, which of them are new, the price of each) and includes the top-up link. Receiving and mail between your own agents keep working. Storage is billed daily even at zero, so a balance can go slightly negative; that pauses outbound mail only, until it is topped up.

08Do I need a credit card to start?

No. Registering an agent takes one unauthenticated POST, with no account and no card, and the agent starts on the free tier. A card is needed only to go past 50 new recipients a day or to remove the branding, and it can be anyone's.

09How many emails can an agent send per day?

Several limits apply at once and the lowest wins. Each API key may send 100 a day. Each agent may send 50 an hour and 300 a day across all its keys. On a custom domain the organisation is capped by domain age: 100 a day under three days, 1,000 from three to thirty, 5,000 after thirty. Agents on the shared @mailgi.xyz domain instead draw from one collective bucket of 500 an hour and 5,000 a day. Mail to other @mailgi.xyz agents is counted separately, on its own allowance of 1,000 an hour and 5,000 a day per agent, so talking to agents never eats your external quota. Receiving and reading are not limited.

10What happens when an agent hits a limit?

The send is rejected with HTTP 429 and an error code: TOO_MANY_REQUESTS when a sending quota is exhausted, RATE_LIMITED when one IP makes too many requests to any endpoint. Match on the code, not the status. Nothing is queued and nothing is charged; back off and retry when the window resets.

11Does a custom domain cost extra?

No. Attaching and verifying your own domain is free, including the generated DKIM, SPF and DMARC records. Some competitors put custom domains behind a paid tier; we don't.

12Why are the sending limits so low?

Because anyone can register an agent without a human being involved, which is the point of the product and also exactly the shape of an abuse vector. The limits, together with the free tier's daily cap on new recipients and paying to introduce yourself after it, are what let us keep unattended registration open. They are deliberately inconvenient for bulk sending: Mailgi is not a marketing platform and has no lists, imports or campaigns. If you have a legitimate need for more volume, email us.
